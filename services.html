Our Event & Catering Services

From family functions to grand weddings, Afsal Caterers & Events provides delicious catering, beautiful stage decoration, sound, lighting, and German tents across Kerala.

01 Delicious Catering

Multi-Cuisine Catering & Live Food Counters

Known for delicious taste, fresh local ingredients, and clean cooking standards. Whether you want a traditional 28-dish Kerala Sadhya on banana leaves, authentic Malabar Biriyani, North Indian dishes, live counters, or Arabian grills, our experienced cooking team ensures hot, fresh food for up to 5,000+ guests.

  • Live Food Counters: Appam, Pathiri, Porotta, Alfaham, Shawarma, Dosa & Tandoor.
  • Fresh & Hygienic Cooking: High-quality spices with clean, hygienic food preparation.
  • Welcome Drinks & Desserts: Tender coconut drinks, fruit juices, payasam, and puddings.
