Our Story

Delicious Food & Complete Event Management

Launched in 2000, Afsal Caterers was founded by Mr. Ashker C.H., who gained extensive experience by working with various leading catering companies. Afsal Caterers is renowned for high quality, excellent customer relations, and professional service across Kerala.

With a wide range of choices, Afsal Caterers & Events offers customisable menus ranging from themed to traditional concepts — Regional, North & South Indian, Chinese, Arabian, and more.
