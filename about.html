About Afsal Caterers

Over 25 years of dedication to delicious food, warm hospitality, and memorable celebrations across Kerala.

Founded in 2000

Passionate Excellence in Every Dish and Detail

Launched in 2000, Afsal Caterers was founded by Mr. Ashker C.H., who gained extensive experience by working with various leading catering companies. Afsal Caterers is renowned for high quality, excellent customer relations, and professional service, making it one of the most trusted and efficient catering companies in Kerala.

With a wide range of choices, Afsal Caterers & Events offers customisable menus ranging from themed to traditional concepts:

Regional, North & South Indian, Chinese, Arabian, etc.

“We excel in understanding our customers' ideas and objectives, creating customisable menus and tailored event plans.”

Founder & Leadership

Over 25 Years of Hands-On Experience

Before starting Afsal Caterers in 2000, Mr. Ashker C.H. worked closely with several leading catering companies, gaining deep hands-on experience in cooking and event management. His goal was to bring authentic Kerala taste together with modern, professional event standards.

Under his direct management, Afsal Caterers has grown from a dedicated local team in Alappuzha to a trusted catering and event management partner across Kerala, serving institutions, corporate clients, and thousands of happy families.

Why Choose Us

Why Families & Clients Trust Us

Simple values that guide everything we do for your special day.

Uncompromising Quality

We source strictly first-grade farm produce, live sea catches, and cold-pressed oils. No artificial colorings or chemical flavor enhancers are ever permitted in our cooking.

Strict Punctuality

Timing is everything in events. Food is served hot at the exact time planned. Stages, tents, and sound systems are completely set up well before guests arrive.

Friendly Customer Care

We treat every family's celebration like our own. Mr. Ashker C.H. and our event coordinators are always available to help, with clear pricing and no hidden costs.

Custom Planning

Every menu, stage design, flower decoration, and schedule is customized to match your budget, family traditions, and personal preferences.
